Help & System Overview

PharmaFlow AI Procurement Assistant

1. Handwritten Want Slips: Upload photos of handwritten doctor/pharmacy orders. PharmaFlow reads handwriting via Sarvam OCR AI and normalizes drug names.

2. Catalogue Matching: Every item is safely matched against our 84-item medicine master dataset. Uncertain items are highlighted for 1-click review.

3. Supplier Comparison: System automatically queries 4 distributor networks and ranks options by full order coverage, quickest delivery (ETA), and best price.

4. Automated Invoice Check: Generating an invoice triggers automatic line-by-line reconciliation checking quantities, rates, and batch numbers.
